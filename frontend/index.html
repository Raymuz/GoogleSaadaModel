How to Use GoogleSaada

Welcome to the GoogleSaada V8 Prediction Engine. This system preserves 13 serialized football research models. Prediction availability depends on verified historical data and supported markets.

Progressive Disclosure System

To protect your daily quota of 20 credits, this interface uses Progressive Disclosure. Prediction buttons (like "Analyze/Predict My Matches" or "Analyze Selected Matches") are intentionally hidden until you have explicitly searched for and loaded scheduled fixtures. Browsing the fixture explorer is entirely free; executing the models consumes quota.

Available Markets & Mixes

  • Mix / Best Outcome: The system automatically selects the highest confidence prediction across all active markets.
  • Full-Time Result (1X2): Probabilities for Home Win, Draw, Away Win.
  • Both Teams to Score (BTTS): Yes/No probabilities regarding offensive capabilities.
  • Over/Under 2.5 Goals: Likelihood of a high-scoring (3+) vs low-scoring (<3) match.
