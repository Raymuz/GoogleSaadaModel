# How to Use GoogleSaada

**Your complete guide to AI-powered football match analysis**

Welcome to GoogleSaada. The platform helps you find scheduled football matches, select prediction markets, and understand estimates generated by eligible statistical and machine-learning models. **Predictions are estimates, not guaranteed results.**

## 1. Getting Started

Open **⚽ Predict Matches**. Sign in if required for a restricted function. Check any displayed usage allowance. Type a match request or use the upcoming-fixture selector. **Up to 16 matches per request** are supported at the request boundary; your remaining quota may be smaller.

GoogleSaada includes six tabs: **Predict Matches**, **Recommendations**, **GoogleSaada Agent**, **Daily Fixtures**, **Live Scores & Results**, and **How to Use GoogleSaada**. Certain Agent features may remain restricted or unavailable.

## 2. Two Ways to Find Matches

### Ask in natural language

In **What matches would you like to predict?**, try:

- `Predict Arsenal vs Chelsea.`
- `Find 10 upcoming matches and rank them by Over 2.5 probability.`
- `Find 5 matches tomorrow and analyze Both Teams to Score.`
- `Analyze 16 upcoming matches and show full-time predictions.`

A request is not evidence that an actual future fixture exists. GoogleSaada must resolve names and confirm source-provided schedules before it can show a prediction. If a team or match is ambiguous, specify its competition and date. Date and ranking intent must be supported by the currently connected workflow; otherwise the application must say the request cannot be completed as stated.

### Choose from scheduled fixtures

Select **1–16** under **Number of matches**. Optionally search for a team or competition. Choose **Select requested games**, review the selected fixtures, and select **Analyze My Matches**. GoogleSaada should attempt all selected eligible matches; it must identify any fixture without sufficient verified evidence rather than insert a fabricated probability.

## 3. Advanced Fixture Search

Expand **Advanced Fixture Search** to reveal the calendar **Game date**, **League**, and **Your time zone**. Selecting a date, league, or timezone requests an updated schedule from the connected fixture provider. Use **Refresh scheduled games** to retry, **Today** to return to the current local date, and **Clear selection** to remove selected fixtures. Changing the selected date may clear fixtures that no longer match.

The provider may not cover every league or may be temporarily unavailable. A scheduled fixture displayed by the provider is not itself an approved historical model identity. Kickoffs are shown in your chosen timezone.

## 4. Analysis Preference

The available settings are **Low**, **Medium**, **High**, and **Balanced**. These govern the configured analysis style or risk selection; they do not guarantee higher model accuracy. **Balanced** is a reasonable initial selection. A model probability is not an assurance of the match outcome.

## 5. Prediction Types and Markets

Choose the markets available in **Choose prediction types**:

- **Full-time result (1X2):** probability of Home Win (1), Draw (X), and Away Win (2). These three probabilities should total approximately 100%.
- **Over/Under goals:** likelihood of the match having more or fewer than a specified number of goals, including Over 2.5 where eligible.
- **Both Teams to Score (BTTS):** whether each team scores at least once.
- **Correct score:** probabilities of individual scorelines, only when a compatible score distribution and reliability checks are available.
- **Half-time / Full-time:** period-specific outcomes when supported by the original goal-model pipeline.
- **First-half / second-half results, double chance and derived markets:** available only where the validated market calculation supports them.

Not every fixture and model supports every market. Missing market results must not be replaced with guessed numbers.

## 6. Understanding the Prediction Models

The original GoogleSaada model family contains **ten trained model artifacts**: XGBoost, Logistic Regression, Random Forest, Elo-based result estimation, and six Poisson goal models for home/away full-time, first-half, and second-half goals. Additional, validation-selected worldwide models cover **full-time 1X2**, **Over 2.5**, and **BTTS**.

GoogleSaada's planned collaborative architecture includes a **Manager Agent**, **Master Orchestrator**, **Shared Whiteboard**, and **Consensus Review**. These components coordinate data provenance and model output. Their presence does not mean the worldwide models are currently connected to every live fixture. GoogleSaada should only identify as active the model actually used to produce a shown result.

A model doesn't automatically retrain when today's fixture list refreshes. New training or collaboration weights require an independent validation and approval process.

## 7. Unfamiliar Teams and Limited History

Some teams have little or no verified recorded history or never appeared in the original training data. GoogleSaada should identify these situations, use only approved historical evidence and verified pre-kickoff features, and abstain from assigning numerical probabilities when a suitable validated fallback is unavailable. A worldwide team dictionary helps resolve identity; it does not create football statistics.

## 8. Understanding Probabilities

A hypothetical 70% Home Win probability is a model's estimated chance for that particular event, **not** a guarantee. Whether 70% forecasts materialize roughly 70% of the time across similar matches is a question of calibration, assessed across many games.

**Accuracy** describes the percentage of correct classified outcomes. **Calibration** describes how closely estimated probabilities match actual long-run frequencies. Different models can disagree because they analyze distinct targets and feature sets. The coordinator can report disagreements; combining predictions is appropriate only when the combination rule has been validated.

## 9. Explanations and Data Provenance

Where available, a grounded explanation may refer to historically observed team strength, pre-match Elo, goal records, home/away form, rolling match statistics, and eligible league patterns. It must never claim to have checked an injury, lineup, live weather, odds or result unless that source information was genuinely retrieved and verified.

Model estimates for match outcome, goals and exact score may come from separate models and should not automatically be described as a single mathematically coherent joint forecast.

## 10. Predicting Multiple Matches and Ranking

You may submit **one to sixteen** fixtures at once. A request to `Find 10 upcoming matches and rank them by Over 2.5` should produce a ranked list only if the source fixture list, eligibility checks, compatible model features, and market-specific ranking all succeed. The number of matches actually predicted may be smaller when evidence is unavailable. Do not interpret a higher probability as a guaranteed or risk-free match.

## 11. Accounts, Credits and Paid Access

Some functions may require authentication or may be subject to daily allowances. When quota information is displayed, review your remaining usage before making a request. Failed or undisplayed analyses should not be charged as successful outcomes; if the displayed balance looks wrong, contact the application administrator. Paid-Agent functions are governed by separate access and security controls and may be disabled.

## 12. Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I analyze 20 games in one request?** No. The intended maximum is sixteen.

**Why can't I find a scheduled match?** Check the selected day, league, timezone, team spelling, and whether the fixture provider covers that competition.

**Can unfamiliar teams be predicted?** Only when the active model has a verified, compatible pre-match feature set and applicable eligibility rules.

**Does High analysis preference make a model more accurate?** No guarantee exists. It changes the configured analysis settings, not established model performance.

**Do the 13 models all predict every match?** No. Each specialist has distinct markets and required features. The orchestrator uses only eligible specialists.

**Does the GoogleSaada Agent guarantee winning predictions?** No. Agent explanations do not override model outputs or release restrictions.

**Can I view first-half, second-half or correct-score markets?** Only when the original goal system has enough verified evidence and the corresponding markets are enabled and validated.

## 13. Troubleshooting

**No games found:** change the date or league, check timezone, and refresh.

**Team not recognized:** add competition and country; do not assume a similar club is the intended one.

**Prediction unavailable:** there may be insufficient history, failed team identity validation, an unsupported market, or a temporary source issue.

**Too many fixtures selected:** reduce to sixteen or fewer.

**Sign-in or credit problem:** use the current account workflow or contact the administrator.

**Model results disagree:** different specialists answer different statistical questions; disagreement does not by itself prove an error.

## 14. Responsible Use and Disclaimer

Football outcomes are uncertain. Injuries, lineups, tactical changes, weather and unexpected events may not be available to the model. Model estimates are for analysis and information, not promises of sporting outcomes or financial returns.

**MANDATORY DISCLAIMER:** GoogleSaada predictions are statistical estimates, not guaranteed results. Predictions involving new, unfamiliar or previously unseen teams may rely on limited data and can carry substantially greater uncertainty. Past performance does not guarantee future outcomes. Verify fixtures and consider the risks before making betting or financial decisions.
